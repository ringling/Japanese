Lesson 2 · めがねをかけている人

The person wearing glasses · Describing appearance
Topic 1

Episode 2

About this lesson

Lesson 2 focuses on describing people's appearance — what they look like and what they are wearing. You learn to pick someone out of a crowd: "She's the tall one," "He's the person wearing glasses."

The lesson introduces two new areas: using V-ている as a noun modifier for ongoing actions and states (wearing, eating, standing), and using 〜そう to express a visual impression ("looks fun," "seems serious"). You also learn how adjectives become adverbs with -く / -に.

The episode is set at a party where characters describe each other and try to work out who is who.

Grammar

Four Patterns

V-ている · 〜そう · adverbs
N₁ は N₂ が A です
N₁ has [N₂] that is [A] / describing physical features
N₁（person）は N₂ が イA です → N₂ が イA-い N₁ / N₂ が ナA-な N₁
Used to describe a person's physical features. In a noun-modifying clause the adjective precedes the noun it modifies.
  • 中村なかむらさんのお兄にいさんはせが高たかいです。 Nakamura-san's older brother is tall.
  • あの髪かみが短みじかい女おんなの人ひとです。 She's the woman with short hair.
  • 頭あたまがいい人ひとですね。 She's a smart person, isn't she.
V-ている / V-ている + N
is doing ~ / the person who is doing ~
V-て + いる (casual: V-てる) / V-ている + N（person or thing）
V-ている describes an ongoing action or a resulting state (especially for wearing — かぶっている, はいている, している). When placed before a noun it functions as a relative clause: "the person wearing X."
  • シンさんのお姉ねえさんは赤あかいサリーをきている人ひとです。 Shin-san's older sister is the person wearing the red sari.
  • めがねをかけてる人ひとです。 He's the person wearing glasses. (casual)
  • ぼうしをかぶっている男おとこの人ひとですか。 Is he the man wearing the hat?
イA / ナA + そうです
looks / seems [A] (visual impression)
イA (drop い) + そうです · ナA + そうです · + そうな + N (modifier)
Expresses a visual impression or guess based on what you can see. Not used for いい → よさそう (irregular!) or ない → なさそう.
  • あそこで遊あそんでいる子こどもたちは楽たのしそうです。 The children playing over there look like they're having fun.
  • お兄にいさん、まじめそうな人ひとですね。 Your older brother looks like a serious person.
  • この料理りょうりはおいしそうですね。 This dish looks delicious!
イA-く / ナA-に + V
do V [adverbially] / do V in a [A] way
イA → -く + V / ナA → -に + V · for impression: -そうに + V
Adjectives become adverbs to describe how an action is performed. イA drops い and adds く; ナA adds に. Combined with そう: -そうに describes how something looks while doing the action.
  • パーティーのへやに花はなをきれいにかざりました。 I decorated the room prettily with flowers.
  • 子こどもたちは楽たのしそうに歌うたを歌うたっています。 The children look like they're happily singing songs.
  • 早はやく書かいてください。 Please write quickly.
