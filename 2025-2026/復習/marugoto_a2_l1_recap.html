Lesson 1 · いい名前ですね

That's a good name · Names, origins & meaning
Topic 1

Episode 1

About this lesson

Lesson 1 opens Marugoto A2 with a focus on names and origins. You learn how to explain what a name means, introduce something unfamiliar by its name, and describe people or things using a modifying clause — "the book I recently read," "the country I've been to."

All three patterns centre on the grammar word という, which links a name or description to a noun, and on the plain-form noun modifier, which turns a full sentence into an adjective. These are among the most useful structures in intermediate Japanese.

The episode is set at a first meeting: characters exchange names, ask about meanings, and talk about where they come from.

Grammar

Three Patterns

という · plain form + N
N という いみ
N means … / the meaning of N is …
N + という + いみ（です）
Used when explaining the meaning of a name, word, or expression. という literally links the word to its content; いみ (意味) = "meaning."
  • ゆうこは やさしい子やさしいこという意味いみです。 Yuko means "a kind child."
  • バウムクーヘンは木きのケーキという意味いみです。 Baumkuchen means "tree cake."
  • 「今日きょうは」はどういう意味いみですか。 What does "Konnichi wa" mean?
N₁ という N₂
a/the [N₂] called [N₁]
N₁ + という + N₂
Used when introducing a name that the listener may not know. N₁ is the specific name; N₂ is the category (company, town, person, etc.). という identifies N₁ as the label for N₂.
  • 田中たなかさんはJFフーズという会社かいしゃで働はたらいています。 Tanaka-san works at a company called JF Foods.
  • わたしは神田かんだという町まちに住すんでいます。 I live in a town called Kanda.
  • なにわという町まちに行いったことがありますか。 Have you been to a place called Naniwa?
Plain form + N
the N that [clause] / a N which [clause]
V / イA / ナA / N (plain form) + N
A whole clause in plain form placed before a noun to modify it — the Japanese equivalent of a relative clause ("the book I read," "the person who came"). The verb is always in plain form regardless of the politeness of the rest of the sentence.
  • 最近さいきん読よんだ本ほんは村上春樹むらかみはるきという作家さっかの小説しょうせつです。 A book I recently read is a novel by an author called Murakami Haruki.
  • 私わたしが今いまほしいものは電子辞書でんしじしょです。 What I want right now is an electronic dictionary.
  • 今いままでに行いったことがある国くにはどこですか。 Which countries have you been to so far?
