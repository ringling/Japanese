Lesson 3 · おすすめは何ですか

What do you recommend? · Ordering & suggestions
Topic 2
4 grammar patterns
Episode 3

About this lesson

Lesson 3 moves into restaurants and food. You learn how to ask for and give recommendations, explain dietary restrictions, and order specific quantities. The lesson is Topic 2, so the setting shifts from introductions to everyday transactions.

Key new structures: 〜ので for giving soft, polite reasons; 〜のは〜です for highlighting what you want to say; 〜なら for picking up the other person's topic and making a suggestion; and 〜ずつ for "N each" when ordering equal amounts.

Rupa is a vegetarian, so she and Abe navigate the menu together — a realistic scenario with lots of ので and なら in action.

Grammar

Four Patterns

ので · のは · なら · ずつ
〜ので、〜
because / since ~ (polite reason)
Plain form + ので、S2 · Note: Noun / ナA → なので (not just ので)
Gives a reason or cause in a soft, objective tone. More polite and less assertive than から. Common when explaining dietary restrictions or preferences to a waiter or host.
  • この店みせは有名ゆうめいなので、お客きゃくさんが多おおいです。 Since this restaurant is famous, it has many customers.
  • 私わたしはベジタリアンなので、肉にくは食たべないんです。 Since I'm a vegetarian, I don't eat meat.
  • 今日きょうは寒さむいので、あたたかいものが飲のみたいです。 Since it's cold today, I'd like something warm to drink.
Compare: 〜から is more direct and casual; 〜ので sounds more considerate and is preferred in polite conversation.
〜のは、N です
what [I/one] ~ is N (focus / emphasis)
V / イA / ナA / N (plain form) + のは、N です
Nominalizes a clause and emphasizes it as the subject, equivalent to "What [I like / is delicious / etc.] is N." Useful for giving clear, focused recommendations.
  • 私わたしが好すきなのは、ステーキです。 What I like is steak.
  • 今いま一番いちばんおいしいのは、かきです。 What's most delicious right now is oysters.
  • 私わたしがよく食たべるのは、テリヤキチキンです。 What I often eat is teriyaki chicken.
N₁ なら、N₂ が いいです
If it's N₁ you want, N₂ would be good
N₁（topic from listener）+ なら、N₂ が いいです（よ）
A topic-sensitive conditional: you pick up what the other person just mentioned (N₁) and build a suggestion around it. The feeling is "speaking of N₁, then N₂ is what I'd recommend." Very natural for waiters and helpful friends.
  • 野菜やさいなら、きせつのサラダがいいですよ。 For vegetables, I'd recommend the seasonal salad.
  • あたたかいものなら、コーンスープがおすすめです。 For something warm, the corn soup is recommended.
  • 冷つめたいものなら、フルーツジュースがおいしいですよ。 For cold drinks, the fruit juice is delicious.
N（数）ずつ
N each / N at a time
Counter + ずつ
Indicates equal distribution — "two of each," "one at a time." Used when ordering the same quantity of multiple items, or when distributing evenly.
  • コーヒーとこうちゃ、2つずつください。 Two each of coffee and tea, please.
  • よせなべと天てんぷら、1つずつお願ねがいします。 One each of hot pot and tempura, please.
  • 毎日まいにちすこしずつ練習れんしゅうしています。 I practise a little every day (a little at a time).
Conversation

Episode 3 Highlight

Key lines
Episode 3 おすすめは何ですか
Rupa
私わたしはベジタリアンなので、肉にくは食たべないんです。 Since I'm a vegetarian, I don't eat meat.
Abe
そうですか。野菜やさいなら、とうふステーキがいいですよ。おいしいので、おすすめです。 I see. For vegetables, the tofu steak is great. It's delicious, so I recommend it.
Rupa
私わたしが好すきなのは、あたたかい料理りょうりです。それと、スープも飲のみたいです。 What I like is warm food. And I'd also like some soup.
Abe
では、とうふステーキとコーンスープ、1つずつお願ねがいします。 Then one each of the tofu steak and corn soup, please.
