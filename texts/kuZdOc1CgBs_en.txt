"The Enchanted Willow"
Long, long ago, in a certain village, there lived a young woman named Oryu.
Every day, Oryu went to the village to work.
Along the way stood a great willow tree.
It was an ancient willow that had stood there for hundreds of years.
Oryu always stopped to rest for a while beneath its branches.
One day, when Oryu reached the willow tree, she found a young samurai standing beneath it.
"Hello."
The samurai greeted her gently.
The next day, the samurai was there beneath the willow again.
From then on, the two met almost every day.
Little by little, they grew to care deeply for each other.
"Let's meet here again tomorrow."
"Yes. See you tomorrow."
But the next day...
The samurai was nowhere to be seen beneath the willow.
He didn't come the following day, either.
"I wonder what happened to him."
One day, news arrived in the village.
The willow tree was to be cut down to provide timber for a large building in the capital.
The men of the village began chopping down the willow.
Clang! Clang!
But the next morning...
The men were astonished.
The cuts they had made the day before had completely healed.
The wood chips had returned to the tree, filling in the cuts.
So the men decided to burn the wood chips.
After that, the cuts no longer healed.
And a few days later...
The great willow finally fell.
"Why has he stopped coming to see me?"
The village men tried to transport the fallen willow to the capital.
But no matter how many people pulled, the willow would not move.
The men went to ask the village elder for advice.
The elder thought for a while, then said,
"Let's ask Oryu to come."
Oryu slowly approached the fallen willow.
Then she gently placed her hand on it.
"Is it you?"
At that very moment, the willow, which had refused to budge until then, slowly began to move.
Oryu finally understood.
The samurai she had met beneath the willow every day...
He had been the spirit of the willow tree all along.
Oryu quietly watched as the willow was taken away.
She never saw the samurai again.
But Oryu never forgot the days she had spent beneath the willow tree.
The End.
