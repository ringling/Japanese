Now, here is 'Momotaro (Japanese Version)'.
Long ago, there was an old man and an old woman.
The old man went to cut wood in the mountains,
and the old woman went to wash clothes in the river.
As she was washing clothes in the river,
a big peach came floating down.
"Oh my, what a big peach!"
The old woman brought the peach home.
When they opened the peach at home... a healthy baby boy came out!
"This is a child given by the gods!"
The old couple raised the child with great care.
His name was Momotaro.
Momotaro grew up strong and kind.
One day, the villagers said,
"The demons on Onigashima are doing bad things."
"I will defeat the demons!"
Momotaro decided to go on a journey.
The old woman made delicious millet dumplings.
Momotaro took the dumplings and set off.
He met a dog, a monkey, and a pheasant on the road.
"Momotaro, where are you going?"
"I’m going to defeat the demons!"
"If you give us dumplings, we will go with you!"
Momotaro shared his dumplings, and they all went together.
When they arrived at Onigashima, there was a big gate.
"Alright, let’s go!"
They all worked together and fought the demons.
The demons said, "We’re sorry! We won’t do bad things anymore!"
The demons handed over their treasure.
Momotaro and his friends returned to the village.
The villagers were very happy.
Momotaro lived happily with his family.
The end.

Strong and kind Momotaro is loved by many people.
